A question I get asked quite often is: What should I eat before and after my workout? So|| I figured out I would write a little post about it. Whether your goal is to build muscle|| lose fat or improve your performance|| what and when you eat impacts your workout and recovery more than we can imagine.\n•\n•\nPRE-WORKOUT:\n👉🏻 The last solid meal should be eaten 2-3 hours prior to working out. A little snack like a fruit with yoghurt|| a toast with peanut butter or a protein shake can be consumed 1-1||5 hours before the training.\n👉🏻 Your pre-workout should consist of carbs (slow and fast)|| protein and a little fat. Eating carbs only may leave you hungry and hypoglycemic by the time you start working out. Adding protein will slow down the digestion of carbs (steady supply of energy for a longer period of time) and provide your muscles with amino acids. Fat will give you that extra kick if you have an intense workout ahead of you.\n•\n•\nPOST-WORKOUT:\n👉🏻The first meal after your workout is very important as your body is in what is known as the ‘anabolic phase’ or the rebuilding phase (it’s time to repair and rebuild these baby muscles).\n👉🏻 Should be consumed within 30 min (1 hour max) post workout. A liquid meal is optimal since it’s easily absorbed and digested.\n👉🏻 The post-workout meal/shake should consist of protein and high glycemic carbs for optimal recovery. The sugar will help raise insulin and lower cortisol and favor muscle building over fat storage.\n👉🏻 A good combination would be whey protein (or any other kind of protein) with a banana or honey.\n•\n•\nFor pre and post workout meal ideas|| check out my stories 🎥 If you have any questions or you want me to write about a certain topic|| comment below 👇🏻☺️\nHave a great day!
